# (Witness) a WITNESS_RECORD session with touch, a lost focus and its resume replays exactly. In Git Bash:
#   WITNESS_RECORD="$TEMP/emb_rec/touch.jsonl" build/embervale.exe --play --seed 5 --script tools/scripts/record/witness_record_touch.txt
#   node ../witness/bin/witness.js replay "$TEMP/emb_rec/touch.jsonl"     (must end in the state the session wrote)
# Kept in tools/scripts/record/ (out of the run_scripts.sh sweep): it needs --play, which a sweep does not pass.
# What it covers: fingers recorded as protocol touch events (the move stick, a world tap), a phone-sized window against
# the replay's 960 x 540 (View::witnessSnap makes the positions come back bit for bit), a lost focus while a finger and a
# key are held (both let go of in the recording and the view; the finger's late lift is dropped), the pause it causes
# (the recorded "pause" action) and the Escape that resumes. Without WITNESS_RECORD it is an ordinary script.
phone
0.5  swipe 100 200 140 190 1.5     # the move stick: a walk to the east
+2.0 swipe 100 200 140 200 3       # a finger on the stick, dragged east (its later moves and its lift come after the resume) ...
+0.0 hold w 2                      # ... and W held: a walk to the north-east
+0.8 blur                          # the window loses the focus: both are let go of, play pauses
+0.5 expect mode paused
+0.3 key Escape                    # resume
+0.2 expect mode play
+0.3 hold d 1.0
+1.5 tap 300 120                   # a world tap away from anyone: a swing
+1.0 quit
